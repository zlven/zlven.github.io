ExProp: Exponential Propagation on Implicit Manifolds for Training-Free DiT Acceleration

Zilin Li, Jinlong Yang, Jinke Wu, Yao Zhou*

Existing feature-prediction methods extrapolate along Euclidean trajectories and ignore the implicit-manifold structure of diffusion trajectories. ExProp reformulates feature prediction as local exponential propagation on the implicit manifold and constrains the geometrically valid neighborhood via a local trust-region mechanism, outperforming existing feature-caching and prediction methods on Flux.1-dev, DiT-XL/2, and HunyuanVideo.

HDS: Heterogeneity-Driven Safeguarding for Accelerating Diffusion Transformers

Zilin Li, Jinlong Yang, Jinke Wu, Yao Zhou*

Existing prediction-based acceleration methods ignore the cross-layer heterogeneity of prediction-error impact and struggle to balance quality and efficiency. HDS is a training-free framework that, based on the discovery of temporally stable inter-layer heterogeneity, evaluates each layer’s risk online with a heterogeneity score (HS): fragile layers keep full computation while robust layers are aggressively accelerated, advancing the quality–efficiency Pareto frontier across DiT architectures at negligible overhead.

FADE: Fractional Anomalous Dynamics Extrapolation for Training-Free Diffusion Transformers Acceleration

Jinlong Yang, Zilin Li, Jinke Wu, Yao Zhou*

Latent trajectories of diffusion Transformers are non-stationary with high curvature, causing error accumulation in integer-order prediction operators. FADE characterizes feature evolution as anomalous diffusion and adopts Mittag–Leffler fractional operators matched to high-curvature nonlinear trajectories, with near-zero-overhead adaptive parameter retrieval at inference via offline multi-dimensional modeling, delivering state-of-the-art speed–fidelity trade-offs across architectures and modalities.

BRACE: Taming Sharp Irregularities via Barycentric Rational Forecasting for Fast Diffusion Transformers Inference

Jinlong Yang, Jinke Wu, Zilin Li, Yao Zhou*

To address the high inference cost of diffusion Transformers and the instability of existing long-step prediction, BRACE proposes a training-free acceleration framework that stably predicts intermediate features via local sliding windows, barycentric rational extrapolation, and adaptive Chebyshev weights, achieving better quality–efficiency trade-offs than prior methods on DiT-XL/2, FLUX.1-dev, and HunyuanVideo.

LaBACK: From Feature Forecasting to Trajectory-Drift Control for Efficient Flow Matching Sampling

Jinlong Yang, Zilin Li, Jinke Wu, Yao Zhou*

Cached features in flow-matching sampling introduce velocity errors that propagate along the sampling trajectory and cause generation drift. LaBACK is a training-free method that reduces approximation error via Laurent-series-guided feature prediction and suppresses error propagation with deterministic backtracking, achieving better speed–quality trade-offs on text-to-image, distilled text-to-image, and text-to-video tasks.
