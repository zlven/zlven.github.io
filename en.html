About

I am currently pursuing a B.S. in Software Engineering at Sichuan University (Class of 2023). My research interests lie in generative models and diffusion models, with a particular focus on discrete diffusion models for text generation.

I maintain the open-source project SEDD-LaTeX-RLHF on GitHub, which aligns discrete diffusion models with RLHF for generating high-quality LaTeX formulas. I am seeking a Ph.D. or Master's position to further pursue impactful research in generative AI.

Open-Source Projects

SEDD-LaTeX-RLHF · Discrete Diffusion + RLHF for LaTeX Generation

Aligns a discrete diffusion model (SEDD) with RLHF via PPO to generate high-quality LaTeX mathematical formulas. Implements a complete SFT → RLHF pipeline, including supervised fine-tuning on the S1K-1.1 math dataset, custom multi-dimensional reward functions (syntax correctness, math quality, exploration bonus), and PPO-based alignment training. Features sparse optimizations for the GPT-2 large-vocabulary setting, enabling single-GPU training.
