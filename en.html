About

I am currently pursuing a B.S. in Software Engineering at Sichuan University (Class of 2023). My research interests lie in generative models and diffusion models, with a particular focus on discrete diffusion models for text generation.

I maintain the open-source project sedd on GitHub, an implementation of discrete diffusion models aimed at providing efficient and well-documented code for the research community. I am seeking a Ph.D. or Master's position to further pursue impactful research in generative AI.

sedd ⭐ [X] · Discrete Diffusion

An open-source implementation of Score Entropy Discrete Diffusion (SEDD) models. This project focuses on applying diffusion models to discrete data such as text, providing a complete pipeline for training, sampling, and evaluation. Includes model architecture, training scripts, and pretrained weights.
