About

I am an undergraduate student (Class of 2023) in Software Engineering at Sichuan University. My research focuses on efficient inference of diffusion models, particularly sampling acceleration and feature caching for Diffusion Transformers (DiT). I have solid foundations in data structures, algorithms, and operating systems, and am proficient in Python and Java.

My work is under review or accepted at AAAI 2027, NeurIPS 2026, and ACM Multimedia 2026, covering fractional-order extrapolation, heterogeneity-driven safeguarding, and implicit manifold propagation for DiT acceleration. I also maintain open-source projects including SEDD-LaTeX-RLHF and EMOcloud. CET-4: 529, CET-6: 469.

I am seeking a Ph.D. or Master's position starting in Fall 2026 to continue research in generative AI.
